This was the day the lights came on. We opened at 8am and stayed open until 6pm, pouring lattes,
dirty chai, and our mushroom coffee from open to close.

Our first day was also a vendor market, with local makers set up inside the shop. Thank you to everyone who showed up, and to the vendors who joined us. Come see the space any day we're open.

## Opening day vendors

- **Forbidden Wing Bookshop**
- **Cozy Relief:** patches, clothing, and prints
- **Roshani Designs:** stickers and prints
- **The Oil Well:** jewelry
- **Within the Oaken Eye:** tarot readings
