Yes, we serve mushroom coffee. No, it doesn't taste like mushrooms.

Ours is simple: one spoonful of chaga powder stirred into an iced latte —
medium $6.75, large $7. Chaga is a birch-forest fungus that people in
Appalachia and far colder places have been brewing into tea for generations.
It doesn't taste "mushroomy" at all — it's smooth and mellow, closer to a
soft cocoa note than anything savory, and it settles beautifully into
espresso and milk.

Why put it on the menu? A few reasons:

- **It fits here.** Foraging, herbalism, and forest tea traditions run deep
  in East Tennessee. A chaga latte belongs in South Knoxville the way
  honey and lavender do.
- **People kept asking for one.** Mushroom coffee has been having a moment
  for years, but in Knoxville it's still surprisingly hard to find made
  fresh at a counter instead of from a packet.
- **It's a gentler ritual.** Folks who want something a little different
  from a straight espresso drink — or who just like trying the weird thing
  on the board — deserve an option that's actually good.

If you're curious, once we're open just ask at the counter and we'll talk
you through it before you commit. And if mushrooms in coffee are simply never going to be your
thing, the rest of the [menu](/menu/) — lattes, dirty chai, red eyes, hot
cocoa — has you covered.

We're on Chapman Highway in South Knoxville — [come try it](/visit/).
