Yes, we serve mushroom coffee. No, it doesn't taste like mushrooms.

Ours is simple: one spoonful of chaga powder stirred into an iced latte:
medium $6.75, large $7. Chaga is a birch-forest fungus that people in
Appalachia and far colder places have been brewing into tea for generations.
It doesn't taste "mushroomy" at all. It's smooth and mellow, with a mild,
earthy note rather than anything savory, and it settles beautifully into
espresso and milk.

Why put it on the menu? A few reasons:

- **It fits here.** Foraging, herbalism, and forest tea traditions run deep
  in East Tennessee. A mushroom coffee belongs in South Knoxville the way
  honey and lavender do.
- **It's worth making fresh.** Mushroom coffee has been having a moment
  for years, and we think it's better made fresh at a counter than stirred
  up from a packet.
- **It's a different ritual.** Folks who want something a little different
  from a straight espresso drink (or who just like trying the weird thing
  on the board) deserve an option that's actually good.

If you're curious, once we're open just ask at the counter and we'll talk
you through it before you commit. And if mushrooms in coffee are simply
never going to be your thing, the rest of the [menu](/menu/) (lattes, dirty chai, red eyes, hot
cocoa) has you covered.
