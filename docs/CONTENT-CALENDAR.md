## Rules of thumb

- 300–600 words. Short, specific, locally grounded.
- Every post links to at least two of: `/menu/`, `/events/`, `/visit/`.
- Write like the shop talks: warm, plainspoken, a little poetic. Never
  "elevated beverage experience."
- Names of real artists/authors/streets/neighborhoods beat adjectives.
- Post on the site first; social posts point AT the site.
